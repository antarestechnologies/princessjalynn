# Moving fans from OnlyFans

No scraping, no automated import, no fan data copied from OnlyFans. The creator's content
comes from her own original files. Fans create their own accounts here.

## Before announcing anything

1. **Check OnlyFans' current terms of service and creator guidelines** about promoting other
   sites. Links or calls to action pointing fans to another platform may breach them and risk
   the OnlyFans account and any pending payout. If they do, announce only on her own channels
   (social profiles, mailing list, link-in-bio page), not inside OnlyFans posts or DMs. Have
   the attorney confirm.
2. Withdraw the OnlyFans balance first, so nothing is at risk if the account is restricted.
3. The new site must be live, processor-approved and tested end to end with a real card
   (see the launch checklist) before any fan is invited.

## Content

- Export her originals from her own storage, not from OnlyFans.
- Before uploading: every item needs its 2257 link (performer record + production date). In
  the post editor, "Link all unlinked items" links a whole post in one step. Items without a
  link cannot be published and stay hidden from fans.
- Suggested order: 20 to 40 strong posts for launch day, then backfill older material through
  scheduled posts over the following weeks so the feed keeps moving.

## Promo window

The site has no discount codes yet. Options, from simplest:

1. **Launch at a lower monthly price** for the first two to four weeks
   (`SUBSCRIPTION_PRICE_CENTS`). Existing subscribers keep the price they joined at, because
   price is stored per subscription.
2. **Free launch posts.** Mark a few posts "free preview" so new members see value before paying.
3. Founding-member pricing or codes would be a small Phase 7 feature if wanted.

## Announcement copy (draft for the creator to edit)

> **I have my own site now.** Same me, more of what you actually come for, and more of your
> money reaches me instead of a platform fee.
>
> **[site address]**
>
> - Monthly membership: $[price]/month. Cancel any time from your account, no questions asked.
> - It's private: your card statement shows "[BILLING_DESCRIPTOR]", and emails say nothing about what the site is.
> - Every member verifies their age once with an independent provider. I never see your ID.
>
> For launch, the first [N] weeks are $[launch price]/month, locked in for as long as you stay
> subscribed.

Keep the announcement free of explicit content and of any claim the attorney has not
approved. "More of your money reaches me" is accurate only if fees really are lower. At
current scale the Phase 0 research found processor fees are about the same as OnlyFans' 20%,
so consider dropping that line.

## How a fan re-subscribes

1. Visit the site and pass the 18+ notice.
2. Create an account (email, handle, password) and confirm the email.
3. Verify age once with the third-party provider.
4. Subscribe on the processor's hosted checkout.
5. The membership is active immediately and a discreet receipt arrives by email.

## After launch

- Watch `/admin/revenue` daily for the first month, especially the chargeback rate. Refund
  quickly and generously; a refund costs far less than a chargeback.
- Keep OnlyFans running in parallel until the new site's subscriber count makes it safe to stop.
